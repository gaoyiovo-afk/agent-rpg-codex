# Fast-Start Worlds

Use these as prompts, not fixed canon. Ask the player to choose or replace one detail before play so the world becomes theirs.

## Rain over the Seventh Pier — cyberpunk noir

- **Premise:** A missing courier sent the protagonist a key that unlocks memories rather than doors.
- **Factions:** municipal memory bureau; dockworkers' mutual-aid network; a clinic erasing corporate witnesses.
- **Opening pressure:** `evidence_decay` clock, 4 segments.
- **Useful stats:** Cool, Insight, Interface, Muscle, Contacts.

## The Bell beneath Ash Abbey — folk horror

- **Premise:** A bell buried for three centuries rings whenever a villager dreams of the same drowned road.
- **Factions:** abbey custodians; families descended from the original burial party; something speaking through the well.
- **Opening pressure:** `third_bell` clock, 6 segments.
- **Useful stats:** Resolve, Lore, Empathy, Cunning, Vigor.

## The Crown without a Shadow — fantasy intrigue

- **Premise:** During the coronation, every torch casts a shadow except the new monarch.
- **Factions:** royal household; oath-bound mage guild; border army awaiting unpaid wages.
- **Opening pressure:** `coup` clock, 6 segments.
- **Useful stats:** Steel, Grace, Wit, Lore, Influence.

## Last Train to Morning — surreal slice of life

- **Premise:** The final subway train stops at stations belonging to lives the passengers almost lived.
- **Factions:** conductors who preserve the timetable; passengers who want to remain; inspectors deleting impossible stations.
- **Opening pressure:** `sunrise` clock, 4 segments.
- **Useful stats:** Nerve, Kindness, Memory, Curiosity.

## Initialization pattern

After the user chooses details, translate the result into an `init` command and then create the opening NPC, first quest, and pressure clock with the state tools. Do not copy a template's hidden explanation into the campaign unless it emerged during play.
